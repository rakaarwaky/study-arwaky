# F3 — Molecular Cell Biology — Field Doc

> One specific sub-field. ~26 h over the quarter (4 sessions × 2 h).
> **Unlocks:** Genetics deep dive + Biochemistry (Q2).
> **No context switching:** this field only on your assigned day.

## Why this slice
The cell is the unit of all life. Get structure + membrane + transport down and genetics/biochem become readable.

## Resources
**Khan Academy — Biology: Cell structure & function** (primary).
**CrashCourse Biology** episodes on cells (fast review layer).
**Molecular Biology of the Cell** (free online edition) — reference only.

## 2-hour daily template
- Input: learn the day's topic (1–2 focused videos + read, this field only).
- Solve: **Draw** a cell & a membrane from memory; label parts; 5 recall questions.
- Essay: write an essay on what I learned.
