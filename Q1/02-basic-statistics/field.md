## Why this slice
Descriptive statistics, probability, and inference are the tools that let you read
and trust data. Without them, any data-driven decision is a guess.

## Resources
- **Khan Academy — Statistics & Probability** (main track).
- **3Blue1Brown — *Statistics*** (intuition layer).
- **Khan Academy — "How to interpret a p-value"** for inference.
- **roadmap.sh Basic Statistics** — the full tree is `roadmap-sh-basic-statistics.md`.

## Exit test
Given a table of numbers, hand-compute mean and SD, state what the SD tells you,
explain the difference between correlation and causation in 2 sentences, and build
one confidence interval.
