## Why this slice
Descriptive statistics, probability, and inference are the tools that let you
read and trust data. Without them, any data-driven decision is a guess.

## Resources
- **Khan Academy — Statistics & Probability** (main track).
- **3Blue1Brown — *Statistics*** (intuition layer).
- **Khan Academy — "How to interpret a p-value"** (inference).

## What you actually finish this quarter
You study statistics on one of the seven rotation days, so that is one
two-hour session a week. Over twelve weeks that is twelve sessions.

Work the sections in order S01 to S06 as a pipeline. You will get through the
first half in this quarter and carry the rest into Q2. Two-sample inference,
ANOVA, regression, and nonparametric tests are the Q2 backlog.

## Exit test
Given a table of numbers, hand-compute mean and SD, state what the SD tells
you, explain the difference between correlation and causation in two
sentences, and build one confidence interval.
