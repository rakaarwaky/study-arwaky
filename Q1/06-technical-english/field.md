# F6 — Technical English — Field Doc

> One specific sub-field. ~26 h over the quarter (4 sessions × 2 h).
> **Unlocks:** Reading a research paper + CS docs & communication (Q2).
> **No context switching:** this field only on your assigned day.

## Why this slice
You're an Indonesian learner — English is your highest-leverage skill. Turn passive consumption into deliberate reading & writing so you can read a paper, write clear notes, and explain ideas in English.

## Resources (free)
The other 6 Q1 fields *are* your reading material (all in English).
**BBC Learning English** (grammar/vocab) — 15 min/day layer.
**Grammarly** (free tier) — check written notes.
One book/month on a Q1 topic, read in English.

## 2-hour daily template (F6 only)
- 0:00–0:05 — State goal: "By end I can explain X."
- 0:05–1:00 — Input: 1–2 focused videos + read (this field only).
- 1:00–1:50 — Write a 1-page English summary of what you read (active recall); record 1–2 min speaking.
- 1:50–2:00 — Write 2–3 sentences (in English) on what I learned + 1 gap.
