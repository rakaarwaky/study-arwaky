# F5 — Classical Mechanics — Field Doc

> One specific sub-field. ~26 h over the quarter (4 sessions × 2 h).
> **Unlocks:** Electromagnetism (Q2).
> **No context switching:** this field only on your assigned day.

## Why this slice
Forces, energy, and momentum are the core of physics. Master these and you can read any 'intro physics' and understand what a machine or body is doing. Uses F1 vectors heavily.

## Resources
**Khan Academy — Physics: Forces & Motion** (primary).
**3Blue1Brown / Walter Lewin lectures** — intuition layer.
Practice: any physics problem set.

## 2-hour daily template
- Input: learn the day's topic (1–2 focused videos + read, this field only).
- Solve: 4–5 problems — free-body diagram, answer, show work.
- Essay: write an essay on what I learned.
