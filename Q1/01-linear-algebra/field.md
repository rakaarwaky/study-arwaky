# F1 — Linear Algebra — Field Doc

> One specific sub-field. ~26 h over the quarter (4 sessions × 2 h).
> **Unlocks:** Machine Learning (Q2) + Software Engineering (Q3).
> **No context switching:** this field only on your assigned day.
> Structure = **roadmap.sh Linear Algebra tree** (linear-algebra-h4gkg), 12 sections.

## Why this slice
Vectors, matrices, and linear transformations are the actual math that powers
software, data, and ML. You can't understand an ML model without this.

## Resources
- **3Blue1Brown — *Essence of Linear Algebra*** (YouTube, 15-part). Primary.
- **Khan Academy — Linear Algebra** (adaptive, problem sets).
- **MIT 18.06 (Gilbert Strang)** — optional depth.
- **roadmap.sh Linear Algebra** — the full tree is `roadmap-sh-linear-algebra.md`.

## Core vs Stretch
- **Core spine (must do in Q1):** S01–S08. This unlocks Q2 (ML + software).
- **Stretch (do in later quarters):** S09–S12 (Advanced Topics, Numerical Methods,
  Applications, Computational). Keep the folders; pull from them once S01–S08 is solid.

At 4 sessions/week you can only tick through **S01–S08** in one quarter. Work S09–S12
as "extra" sessions when ahead, or as the opening of Q2.

## Exit test
Explain what a linear transformation is and what an eigenvalue means, from memory,
in 3 sentences each; explain what the SVD does and why it matters for ML; solve 1
mixed system without hints.
