# F7 — Personal Finance — Field Doc

> One specific sub-field. ~26 h over the quarter (4 sessions × 2 h).

## Why this slice
Compounding, budgeting, and risk are the personal-finance core. Master these and investing and macroeconomics become understandable — and you'll actually use this for life.

## Resources (free)
**Khan Academy — Economics: Personal finance / Investing** (primary).
**The Psychology of Money** (Morgan Housel) — mindset layer.
**Investopedia** — reference for any term.

## 2-hour daily template (F7 only)
- 0:00–0:05 — State goal: "By end I can explain X."
